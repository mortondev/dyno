# Gauntlet round 1: independent judge report

Judged from the 18 paired images only (game left, concept right); all 18 in-game shots were also checked at 1280x720. Layout and camera distance are not penalised; look and proportion are.

Water is scored only where the in-game shot shows water: five locations. The other 13 are n/a. For seven of those the concept has a river, pond or lake in frame (ashcloak_stockade, barrow_crypt, logging_camp, miners_rest, old_barrow, windmill_farm, hearthstead_dusk); that is noted as an observation and not scored anywhere, since it may be a layout difference.

## 2. Gaps per criterion

**Lighting (4.89).** Sun shadows exist and are crisp (the tree shadow at hollow_oak, roof shadows at fishers_landing and miners_rest), and lamp light on nearby surfaces is good at fishers_landing and old_barrow. But the targets set a warm key light against cool shadow, and the game's daylight is nearly uniform: grass is the same neon green in sun and shade, with little visible ambient occlusion where grass meets walls, rocks and trunks. Windows are dark on every building, including at dusk (hearthstead_dusk, fishers_landing), where the targets have warm lit windows and lantern pools along the road. Night and dusk scenes (ashcloak_stockade, barrow_crypt) are lit by oversized soft glowing discs, with no moon or moonlit rim on trees and roofs.

**Atmosphere (4.39).** The game does have far hills and mountains (miners_rest, logging_camp, hearthstead_dusk), but they render as a washed-out, very low-resolution pale band: big flat slabs, some detached and floating above the horizon (emberford_bridge, sunken_shrine, hearthstead_plaza, wrecked_caravan), and at mirrormere a broken mosaic fills the upper right of the sky. The targets show crisp layered forest, then hills, then mountains, each a step bluer. Time of day is off in places: barrow_crypt is pink dusk where the target is a moonlit blue night, and watch_tower and mirrormere are flat noon where the targets are sunset. Clouds are a few large flat slabs, not many small puffy cumulus. hollow_mines shows no sky at all.

**Voxel quality (4.33).** Props, buildings and grass tufts are crisp voxels, but the ground is not: cobbles, sand and rock are a noisy per-pixel texture at a much finer grain than everything else (hearthstead_plaza, fishers_landing, wrecked_caravan), and the hollow_mines hillside is a smeared, stair-stepped surface with banding. Roads carry two clear artefacts in eight shots: raised pale paving slabs with light-blue outlines scattered over the cobbles, and flat brown splats lying on the road. Thatch and tile roofs are a fine streaky texture, not chunky voxel courses, and the plaster walls at hearthstead_dusk look smeared. Voxel scale differs between leaf crowns (very coarse), props (medium) and ground (very fine).

**Water (3.60).** Water is an opaque flat blue sheet with a faint tile pattern at emberford_bridge, mirrormere and fishers_landing: no reflections of sky, trees or lamps, and no sun or sunset glitter path. sunken_shrine is the best, with lighter turquoise shallows and sparkle around the pillars, and mirrormere has reeds, ducks and lily pads. Still missing against the targets: a bright foam line on every shore, see-through shallows showing the bed, waterfalls, and stepped stone banks (emberford_bridge has a dark block wall for a bank). The hollow_oak pond is barely visible.

**Vegetation (3.83).** Most tree crowns are single dark, coarse masses on straight poles, nearly black-green against neon grass; the targets have bright multi-clump crowns with lit tops, thick tapering trunks with root flare, and branches. Birches are a thin white pole holding one ball (mirrormere, fishers_landing, north_watch). Species variety is good at north_watch and logging_camp and thin elsewhere. Ground cover is the reverse problem: knee-high grass, lupins and poppies carpet everything at uniform density and bury paths and props (logging_camp, miners_rest, north_watch), where the targets have short turf with scattered tufts, flower clusters and mossy boulders. Shrubs are smooth rounded green lumps. Bare leaning poles stand in for dead trees at hollow_oak and barrow_crypt.

**Scale and proportion (3.94).** Buildings are near heroic size (the Hearthstead tower, the houses on hearthstead_street, the two-storey house at miners_rest). Landmarks are the weak point: the windmill has tiny sails for its tower, the Hollow Oak is only a little bigger than its neighbours with no visible hollow or door, the logging camp has two small sheds and no mill, and the mine has a small doorway and a small headframe. The plaza and Fisher's Landing go the other way: very large bare fields of cobble and sand against the targets' modest, bordered spaces. Around most landmarks the ground is a level plain (wrecked_caravan, windmill_farm, ashcloak_stockade, emberford_bridge), where the targets roll.

**Life (3.67).** There is life: NPCs in the plaza, a fisher at mirrormere, deer at emberford_bridge, rabbits at logging_camp, a sheep at windmill_farm, ducks on the water, fireflies at dusk. What is missing is chimney smoke (none in any shot), campfires, readable gold "!" markers, enemies in the camps (no bandits at ashcloak_stockade, no spider at hollow_oak, no boar at logging_camp), and NPCs visibly doing a job. Birds are overdone: flocks of 10 to 40 dark shapes in almost every sky (sunken_shrine, mirrormere, windmill_farm), where the targets have two or three. Fireflies, wisps and butterflies are oversized (barrow_crypt, ashcloak_stockade, sunken_shrine).

**Composition and readability (3.56).** The targets each have a path leading from the character to one clear landmark, framed by a foreground tree. Several game shots have no readable landmark: ashcloak_stockade is the back of a see-through palisade with no gate, mirrormere looks at a single tree and an empty beach, hollow_mines looks down a slope at a tiny entrance, fishers_landing is dominated by a roof and a blank sign, and old_barrow views the crypt from behind and above. Clutter adds noise: the floating horizon slabs, road splats and outlined slabs, a tree floating above the hill at logging_camp, and tall grass burying props.

## 3. Ten changes ranked by impact

1. **light: fix the far distance.** Draw far hills and mountains as continuous, crisp, layered silhouettes that step into blue aerial haze, and remove the floating pale slabs and the mirrormere sky mosaic. Affects every daytime shot.
2. **terrain: make the ground surface voxel-true.** Cobble, dirt, sand and rock at the same 25 cm grain as the props, with no fine noise texture and no smeared stair-stepping (hollow_mines); remove the outlined raised slabs and brown splats from roads.
3. **vegetation: rebuild tree crowns and trunks.** Multi-clump crowns in brighter, sunlit greens with per-voxel variation, thick tapering trunks with root flare and branches, proper birches and willows. Remove the ball-on-a-pole look and the bare leaning poles.
4. **vegetation: thin and shorten the ground cover.** Short turf with scattered tufts, flower clusters and mossy boulders; keep paths and props clear; shift the grass from neon green to warmer, more varied greens.
5. **light: warm/cool grade, AO and emissive light.** Warm sun against cool shadow, contact AO under grass, eaves and props, lit windows and lantern pools with gentle bloom, a moon and moonlit rim at night, and each location set to its target time of day (barrow_crypt night; watch_tower and mirrormere sunset).
6. **world: build and dress the landmarks.** A stockade gate with banners, tents and a fire; large windmill sails; a giant Hollow Oak with a doorway; a sawmill with a water wheel; a mine portal with rails and a tall headframe; a jetty, boats and nets at Fisher's Landing; banners on the towers. Each should be the largest shape in its shot, with a road leading to it.
7. **water: reflections, foam and clarity.** Sky, tree and lamp reflections with a sun or moon glitter path, a foam line on every shore, see-through shallows, and stepped stone-and-reed banks.
8. **life: swap the bird swarms for grounded life.** Two or three birds per sky, chimney smoke on occupied buildings, campfires, smaller fireflies and butterflies, enemies in the camps, NPCs at work, and a gold "!" on quest givers.
9. **world: tighten the open spaces and plant framing trees.** Shrink the plaza cobble field and the Fisher's Landing sand field to modest spaces bordered by buildings, stalls, fences and trees, and put a large oak or birch beside each approach.
10. **world: roll the ground around landmarks.** Gentle 15-35 m hills and a meadow-then-wood rhythm in place of the level plains at wrecked_caravan, windmill_farm, ashcloak_stockade and emberford_bridge.

## 4. Worst and best

**Worst**

1. **hollow_mines (3.00).** No sky or distance at all, a smeared and banded hillside, a small mine door and headframe, no rails, carts or lantern-lit portal; nothing of the target's cliff face and valley view.
2. **ashcloak_stockade (3.57).** The back of a lattice palisade you can see through, with no gate, banners, fire or enemies, on a level plain under a moonless sky; the target is a lit camp with a tower behind.
3. **mirrormere (3.62).** No landmark in view: one ball-on-a-pole birch, an empty beach and flat opaque water, under the worst sky artefacts of the set (a broken mosaic across the upper right) and a swarm of birds; the target is a sunset lake road to a lit lodge.

fishers_landing (3.75) is just behind: a very large bare sand field, one looming streaky thatch roof, a blank white sign and flat water with no jetty, boats, smoke or sunset reflection. Its lamp light is the one strong point.

**Best**

1. **hearthstead_dusk (5.00).** The low sun, pink-to-blue sky, long street towards the tower and fireflies come closest to a target mood; it still lacks lit windows, crisp mountains and lantern glow.
2. **north_watch (4.71).** A clear tower at the end of a path, framing trees on both sides and real species variety; it lacks the autumn palette, the inn, and a visible path through the grass.
3. **emberford_bridge (4.62).** The bridge is a strong, readable, well-built landmark with lamp posts, a figure for scale and deer beyond; the flat water, level far bank and floating horizon hold it back.
