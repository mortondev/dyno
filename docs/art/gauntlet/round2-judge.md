# Gauntlet round 2: independent judge

Judged from the 18 pair images and the 18 full-size in-game shots only. Scores are 1 to 10, where 10 is indistinguishable from the target in that respect. Layout and camera distance were not penalised; look and proportion were.

**Overall average: 4.37 / 10** (mean of all 132 scored cells).

Water rule: scored only where the game shot shows water (6 locations), on its quality. Four targets are built around water that the game shot does not show at all (barrow_crypt, fishers_landing, hollow_oak, windmill_farm); those are n/a because the absence is layout, and they are covered in the notes and in change 5. If those four were scored 1 instead, Water would be 2.80 and the overall 4.27.

## 2. Gaps by criterion

**Lighting (5.33).** Sun shadows exist, and lamp bloom at night is the strongest thing in the set (stockade, dusk street). The target's warm-and-cool contrast is missing by day: grass is a flat, self-lit neon green with almost no shading, where the target has warm olive lit faces and cool blue-violet shadows. Buildings have no lit windows or door glow anywhere, so every house reads cold and empty next to the target's amber windows and lanterns. Ambient occlusion is weak under eaves, at wall bases and around tufts, and the night scenes have no moon and no cool key light: the ground stays saturated green instead of going blue.

**Atmosphere (4.78).** The target always closes with three layers: forest, rolling hills, then blue or snow-capped mountains in haze. The game's distance is a row of big flat-colour blocks (emberford, sunken shrine, mirrormere) in grey-green, beige and even purple slabs, with a hard edge against the sky and no gradient of haze. Clouds are sparse and tiny, and several shots show a stretched cloud band at the top of the frame. Dusk and night skies have a good colour, but there is no sun glow on the land (fisher's landing, mirrormere), no moon, and no ground mist at the barrows.

**Voxel quality (4.06).** The target is uniformly small crisp cubes with per-voxel colour jitter. The game mixes at least three scales: fine tufts, coarse terrain steps, and brick or cobble patterns on walls, kerbs and pillars whose bricks are several times the voxel size. Road surfaces are blurred and smeared (soft patches and green blotches on every cobbled road, worst in plaza, street, caravan, windmill), terrain slopes show stretched stripes (north watch left foreground, the miners' rest dome boulder, the cliff in hollow mines), and tree crowns at the windmill and logging camp carry a blurry mottled leaf texture on huge blocks. Waterfalls are flat noisy white-teal slabs.

**Water (4.00 where shown).** Emberford, mirrormere and the sunken shrine have a decent colour, a shore line and lily pads; mirrormere reflects the far bank and the shrine has a clear-shallows gradient. They lack the target's sun or moon glitter path, lantern reflections, voxel foam at banks and rocks, a visible stepped bed, and cattails at every edge; emberford's river is a flat blue sheet covered in white noise. The two waterfalls (logging camp, miners' rest) are flat textured slabs with no steps or spray, and the stockade's water is a dark patch with no moon reflection. Four further targets are built around a pond, lake or river that the game shot does not show.

**Vegetation (4.11).** Flower and tuft ground cover is the best part and is close in density at the windmill, caravan and old barrow. Trees are the gap: the target frames each shot with a thick, gnarled, rooted trunk and a layered crown, and fills the middle distance with oaks, birches, pines, willows, blossom and fruit trees of mixed sizes. The game has mostly one lollipop oak with a straight trunk, stick-thin birch poles under blob crowns, and solid dark walls of identical trees at the barrows. Path edges are bare: no grass leaning over the kerb, no stone clusters, and emberford's meadows are an empty lawn.

**Scale and proportion (4.50).** Towers, houses and the stockade gate are a believable heroic size against the person. The plaza and the caravan road are the "giant cobbled field" the art direction forbids, and main roads are far wider than the target's 4 to 6 m. The Hollow Oak is a thin pole with a small crown where the target is a tree as wide as a house. The barrow entrances are small boxes instead of a doorway into a broad mound, and the landscape has little relief: flat lawns where the target has rolling hills.

**Life (3.56).** Chimney smoke in Hearthstead, fireflies and motes at night, a few birds and a deer at the hollow oak are present and good. The target has NPCs doing things at every landmark (fishers on the jetty, loggers chopping, miners with carts, merchants at the caravan, farmers and sheep, guards on the tower, a quest giver with a gold "!"), plus ducks, crabs, herons, butterflies, rabbits and campfires. The game's NPCs are few and standing still (one beside the player at emberford, three in the plaza), and the logging camp, caravan and windmill have nobody readable.

**Composition and readability (4.39).** The target always has a path from the character's feet to one landmark, with a framing trunk and a foreground of rocks and flowers. Only the tower shots (watch tower, north watch, dusk) and the stockade achieve that. Elsewhere the view is jammed under an eave or canopy (logging camp), blocked by a foreground pillar or boulder (crypt, miners' rest), looks down onto the landmark's roof with no path to it (old barrow), or the landmark is not readable (hollow oak, the caravan, the mill wheel). Buildings at the frame edges lean and stretch visibly (miners' rest house, logging camp, dusk street).

## 3. Ten changes ranked by impact

1. **[light] Regrade daylight: desaturate and warm the grass, add a cool shadow tint and stronger AO.** The neon flat green is the single biggest difference in all 12 day shots and carries into the 6 dusk and night shots. Aim for the target's olive-yellow lit faces, blue-violet shadows, and visible contact darkening under eaves, tufts and rocks. Add a moon with a cool key light at night.
2. **[light] Distance haze and horizon.** Fade far terrain into blue aerial haze with a soft horizon gradient, tint it toward the sky colour at dusk and night, add more and larger clouds, and add ground mist at the barrows. The far blocks currently meet the sky with a hard edge in every exterior.
3. **[world] Far terrain as layered forest, hills and mountains.** Replace the flat-colour block skyline (beige, grey and purple slabs) with forest-coloured far terrain, a hill layer and a mountain ridge, and give the near landscape rolling relief instead of flat lawn.
4. **[world] Tree shapes and species.** Thick rooted trunks, branching, layered crowns built from small leaf clumps; real birches, pines, willows, blossom and fruit trees in mixed sizes; a framing tree near each landmark. Make the Hollow Oak a true giant with a doorway, roots, banners and mushrooms.
5. **[world] Put water in view where the targets have it.** A pond at the crypt and at the hollow oak, the lake and jetty at Fisher's Landing, a river and stone bridge at Miner's Rest, a mill race and wheel at the logging camp, a river at the windmill.
6. **[terrain] Crisp ground at one voxel scale.** Remove the blurred cobble and green blotches on road surfaces and the stretched stripes on slopes and cliffs; give roads, dirt and grass per-voxel colour variation at the same size as the rest of the world.
7. **[life] Working NPCs and critters at every landmark.** Two to five figures per place with a job (fishing, chopping, mining, guarding, trading), a gold "!" quest giver, campfires with smoke, and ambient creatures (ducks, sheep, deer, crabs, butterflies, rabbits, birds). Day shots have almost no ambient life.
8. **[world] Building, road and landmark dressing.** Lit amber windows and door lanterns, flower boxes, signs, barrels, carts, fences along roads; bricks on walls, kerbs and pillars at voxel scale; main roads narrowed to 4 to 6 m and the plaza shrunk so buildings, stalls and trees border it. The caravan needs a readable wagon with canvas and wheel, the barrows a mound with a proper doorway and gravestones, the mine a tunnel mouth facing the approach.
9. **[water] Surface quality.** Sun and moon glitter path, reflections of lanterns and sky colour, voxel foam along banks and around stones, a visible stepped bed in clear shallows, and waterfalls as stepped voxel cascades with spray instead of flat noisy slabs.
10. **[vegetation] Leaf materials, path edges and foreground.** Replace the blurry mottled leaf texture with clean per-voxel leaf colour; add taller tuft clumps leaning over road edges, flower drifts by colour, mossy boulder clusters without smeared surfaces, and cattails at all water edges. Fill the bare meadows at emberford and north watch.

Characters did not make the top ten. The remaining gap there is silhouette: the target's figures have oversized shoulders, weapons and cloaks with clear colour blocking and active poses, where the game's read as small plain blocks standing still.

## 4. Worst and best

**Worst**

1. **logging_camp (2.75).** The view is wedged under a dark roof and a yellow canopy, a flat white-teal waterfall slab cuts the frame in half, the mill and wheel are unreadable, and there is not one NPC or creature against a target full of loggers, a boar fight and a turning wheel.
2. **miners_rest (3.50).** No river, bridge or headframe in view; a leaning two-storey house and a striped smeared dome boulder dominate; another slab waterfall on a grey rock; the target's low sun and mine activity are absent.
3. **barrow_crypt (4.14, tied with wrecked_caravan).** The target is a moonlit approach to a doorway in a mound; the game shows a small lit box behind a foreground pillar, with neon green ground at night, no moon, a wall of identical trees and blocky far terrain. The caravan ties because its landmark is two unreadable plank walls beside a vast road with no figures.

**Best**

1. **hearthstead_dusk (5.57).** Low sun with glow, pink-violet sky, lamp bloom, chimney smoke, fireflies, a tower landmark down a street. It still lacks lit windows, mountains and a crisp road.
2. **watch_tower (5.43).** Clear tower landmark, stalls, smoke plumes, flowers and a readable town; let down by the neon grass, smeared road and noon light where the target is sunset.
3. **ashcloak_stockade (4.88).** Stars, strong lamp bloom, fireflies, a readable gate with banners at the end of a path. It lacks the moon, the campfire and bandits inside, and the grass is still daylight green.
