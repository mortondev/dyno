Art direction for Hearthblock Online: a Cube World x World of Warcraft hybrid MMORPG. It must look FUN and BEAUTIFUL: a world you instantly want to adventure in.
VOXELS: everything is built from voxels at ONE consistent fine scale (about 8 voxels per metre for terrain, buildings, trees and characters alike; finer only for tiny details). Never smooth polygons, never a flat painting.
CUBE WORLD: clean, charming, colourful voxel forms; soft bright light with gentle ambient occlusion; saturated candy-but-harmonious colours; rounded puffy canopies built from many leaf clumps; rolling terrain.
WORLD OF WARCRAFT: heroic chunky fantasy architecture, readable landmarks, strong silhouettes, each zone with its own signature palette and mood, light shafts, aerial haze, distant vistas.
LIFE IS ESSENTIAL: show the world inhabited. Include small voxel adventurers in colourful class gear (a plate warrior, a robed mage casting a glowing spell, a hooded hunter with a bow), townsfolk, a quest giver with a floating gold "!" above them, and creatures fitting the place. Add motion cues: chimney smoke, birds, butterflies, fireflies, falling leaves, glowing magical motes.
WONDER: give every place at least one fantastical, memorable element (glowing runestones, giant mushrooms, an ancient statue, a small waterfall, magical crystals, banners in the wind).
VARIETY: mix tree species, sizes and colours (oaks, birches, pines, blossom trees, autumn tints), flower meadows, rocky outcrops, gentle hills; avoid one repeated tree and empty flat fields.
COMPOSITION: a clear focal landmark, an interesting foreground, a beautiful background vista; never a large blurry object filling the foreground. You may lower and reframe the camera slightly for a more cinematic third-person MMO view, but keep the same location: the same buildings, landmarks, paths and water in the same arrangement as the reference screenshot.
