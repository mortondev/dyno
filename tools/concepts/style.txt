Art direction for Hearthblock Online: a Cube World x World of Warcraft hybrid. The world is built from VOXELS and must read as voxel art, never smooth polygons or a 2D painting.
From Cube World take: the clean, charming voxel look; chunky but finely shaped voxel forms (about 8 voxels per metre for terrain and buildings, finer for small props and details); soft, bright, colourful lighting with gentle ambient occlusion and soft shadows; vivid saturated colour; round, puffy voxel tree canopies built from many voxel leaf clumps; voxel flowers, grass tufts and mushrooms scattered on colourful voxel ground; a cheerful, cosy adventure mood.
From World of Warcraft take: the art direction and world design: heroic, chunky fantasy architecture (timber-framed houses with plaster, steep shingled roofs with deep eaves and moss, stone towers with battlements and banners, arched stone bridges, lanterns on posts), readable landmarks, strong silhouettes, zone moods (warm golden Elwynn-like meadows, eerie Duskwood-like woods at night, misty lakes), warm/cool colour contrast, light shafts, blue aerial haze and distant hills.
Keep the exact layout, landmarks, camera angle and composition of the reference screenshot: same buildings in the same places, same paths, terrain shape and water. Raise the fidelity of everything within the voxel style: richer voxel detail, colour variation, props and dressing, lighting and atmosphere. No text, no UI, no watermark. Wide 16:9 game screenshot.
